(Islamic App)

Everything a Muslim needs through the day, in one app. Read and listen to the Quran, get prayer times and the qibla, follow illustrated stories from the Quran, and keep up your azkar. In English and Arabic.

Everything in one place

Built for daily use, for adults and children, in English and Arabic.

Quran

  • Uthmani script with Saheeh International or Tafsir Al-Muyassar.
  • Nine reciters; the ayah is highlighted as it plays.
  • Search inside the verses, browse by juz, jump to any page.
  • Bookmarks and continue reading.

Memorise and complete

  • Memorisation mode: repeat each ayah, loop a range, hide the text.
  • Khatma planner: a daily portion, progress and streak.
  • Offline: save the whole Quran text on the device.

Prayer

  • Five calculation methods and the Hanafi Asr option.
  • Adhan alerts with a switch per prayer.
  • Prayer tracker and a 7-day timetable.
  • Qibla compass and nearby mosques.

Quran stories

  • Twelve stories, from Adam to Dhul-Qarnayn.
  • Animated scenes with narration, ambience and recitation.
  • Kids mode with simpler words and a quiz.
  • The prophets are never depicted: only places and signs.

Ad'iyah and azkar

  • Hisn al-Muslim in full: 132 chapters with tashkeel.
  • Translation, transliteration and audio for each dhikr.
  • Repeat counters and a tasbih with a running total.
  • Quranic duas straight from the Quran text.

And more

  • Hijri calendar with occasions, fasting days, imsak and iftar.
  • 99 Names of Allah with meanings.
  • Zakat calculator with the nisab worked out.
  • English and Arabic, dark and light, phone to desktop.

Good to know: recitation and adhan audio are streamed and need internet. Adhan alerts work while the app is open or installed, not when it is fully closed. Prayer times are calculated and can differ by a minute or two from a local mosque; Hijri dates follow Umm al-Qura. Installing on a phone, the live compass and GPS need the app hosted on a secure (HTTPS) address. The zakat calculator is a guide only.

Content sources: Quran text, translation and tafsir through alquran.cloud; recitations from islamic.network; adhan audio from aladhan.com; supplications from Hisn al-Muslim through hisnmuslim.com; mosque locations from OpenStreetMap contributors.
