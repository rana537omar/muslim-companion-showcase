## The five essentials

1. **Quran.** All 114 surahs with translation, tafsir and recitation.
2. **Prayer times.** Calculated for your city or your GPS location, with a live countdown.
3. **Qibla.** Direction and distance to the Kaaba, with a live compass on phones.
4. **Quran stories.** Twelve stories told in animated scenes, with narration, ambient sound and recitation.
5. **Ad'iyah and azkar.** The full Hisn al-Muslim with tashkeel, translation and audio.

## Features

### Quran
- **114 surahs** in Uthmani script, with the Saheeh International English translation or the Arabic Tafsir Al-Muyassar.
- **Recitation** per ayah or for a whole surah, with the ayah highlighted as it plays. Nine reciters, including Mishary Alafasy, Al-Husary, Al-Minshawi and Abdul Basit.
- **Search inside the verses** in Arabic or English, browse by **juz**, or jump to any of the **604 pages**.
- **Bookmarks** and **continue reading** from where you stopped.
- **Memorisation mode**: repeat each ayah up to 10 times, loop a range, and hide the text to test yourself.
- **Khatma planner**: choose how many days, get a daily portion, and follow your progress and streak.

### Prayer
- **Prayer times** worked out from your coordinates, with five calculation methods (Egyptian, Muslim World League, Umm al-Qura, Karachi, ISNA) and the Hanafi Asr option.
- **Live countdown** to the next prayer and a **7-day timetable**.
- **Adhan alerts**: a banner, a notification and the adhan at each prayer time, with a switch per prayer.
- **Prayer tracker**: tick off the five prayers and see your last seven days.
- **Qibla compass** with bearing and distance to the Kaaba.
- **Nearby mosques** from OpenStreetMap.

### Quran stories
- **Twelve stories**: Adam, Nuh, Ibrahim, Yusuf, Musa, Yunus, the People of the Cave, the People of the Elephant, Sulaiman, Maryam, Ayyub and Dhul-Qarnayn.
- **Animated scenes** for each part of the story, with ambient sound (rain, sea, wind, fire), narration and recitation of the related verses.
- **Kids mode**: simpler wording and a short quiz after each story.
- **Record your own narration** for any scene, in your own voice.
- **Out of respect, the prophets are never depicted.** The scenes show only places and signs.

### Ad'iyah and azkar
- **Hisn al-Muslim in full**: 132 chapters, fully vowelled, with English translation, transliteration and audio.
- **Morning and evening, after prayer, before sleep and on waking** one tap away, each dhikr with its own repeat counter.
- **Quranic duas** taken directly from the Quran text.
- **Tasbih counter** with targets of 33, 99 and 100 and a running total.

### More
- **Hijri calendar** with Ramadan, the two Eids, Arafah, Ashura and the white days marked, plus imsak and iftar times in Ramadan.
- **The 99 Names of Allah** with meanings.
- **Zakat calculator** that works out the nisab from today's gold or silver price.

### Made for everyone
- **English and Arabic**, with a full right-to-left layout.
- **Dark and light modes** in purple, grey and black.
- **Phone, tablet and desktop** layouts.
- **Installable**, and the whole Quran text can be saved for offline reading.

### Good to know
- Recitation and adhan audio are streamed, so they need an internet connection. The Quran text needs internet the first time each surah is opened, or once if you download it all.
- Adhan alerts work while the app is open or installed. They do not ring when the app is fully closed.
- Prayer times are calculated astronomically and can differ by a minute or two from a local mosque. Hijri dates follow the Umm al-Qura calendar and can differ by a day from local moon sighting.
- Spoken narration uses the voices installed on the device, so its language and quality vary. Recording your own narration works on the computer that runs the app.
- Installing on a phone, the live compass and GPS location need the app to be hosted on a secure (HTTPS) address.
- The zakat calculator is a guide only.

### Content sources
- Quran text, translation and tafsir: Uthmani script, Saheeh International and Tafsir Al-Muyassar, through alquran.cloud.
- Recitations: islamic.network. Adhan audio: aladhan.com.
- Supplications: Hisn al-Muslim by Sa'id al-Qahtani, through hisnmuslim.com.
- Mosque locations: OpenStreetMap contributors.
